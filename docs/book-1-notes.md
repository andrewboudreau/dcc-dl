# Book 1 Notes: *Dungeon Crawler Carl*

Working notes for keeping the game close to the book. These are our own summaries, not quotes.
Each detail is tagged with how sure we are:

- ✅ **confirmed**: found in at least one published summary or excerpt (sources below)
- 🟡 **memory**: what we remember, not yet checked against a source
- ❓ **open**: we don't know yet. Check the book.

## Opening chapters, in order

### Before the collapse (game: `prologue`)
- ✅ Carl is a Coast Guard veteran.
- ✅ He is looking after **Princess Donut**, a prize-winning Persian show cat that belongs to his ex-girlfriend **Beatrice (Bea)**.
- ✅ In the middle of a freezing winter night, Donut jumps out the window and Carl goes outside after her, barely dressed.
- 🟡 Carl spends the series without pants (running joke).
- ❓ Exactly what Carl is wearing (boxers + jacket? which jacket? shoes?).

### The collapse (game: `prologue`)
- ✅ Earth is "transformed" during a winter storm: everything under a roof is destroyed, along with everyone inside. Carl lives because he is outside chasing Donut.
- ✅ One source gives the time as **2:23 AM, January 3rd**.
- ❓ The severed head. We put it in the prologue from memory, but haven't found it in a source. Whose head is it, and where in the chapter?
- ❓ How close Carl comes to freezing before he finds the stairwell.

### Entering the dungeon (game: `prologue` → `stairwell` → `registration`)
- ✅ An alien **Syndicate** has taken Earth. Survivors can go down stairwells into the **World Dungeon**, run by the **Borant Corporation**: 18 floors, broadcast across the galaxy as entertainment.
- ✅ Every floor has a **collapse timer**. Anyone still on the floor when it runs out dies.
- ✅ Carl goes down an ornate staircase into the warm, humid first floor.
- ✅ The surface stairwells close about an hour after the dungeon opens, and the System AI's introductory announcement comes after that.
- ✅ Donut is registered as a crawler too.
- ❓ The Floor 1 timer length.
- ❓ The System AI's name and how it introduces itself. One source mentions an announcer called Cascadia, but we haven't confirmed that.

### Registration: first achievements (game: `registration`)
- ✅ Carl's first achievements come with loot boxes, including:
  - a **Legendary Pet Box** (for bringing a pet, Donut)
  - a **Silver Adventurer Box** (for entering early)
  - a **Bronze Adventurer Box** (for an empty pack)
- ✅ There are also sarcastic achievements for things like bringing a cat, not having suitable clothing, and surviving the first encounters.
- ❓ The exact achievement names and wording. Our game paraphrases them.

### Tutorial Guild and Mordecai (next scene to build)
- ✅ Carl reaches the **Tutorial Guild** and meets **Mordecai**, the game guide. Mordecai was once a crawler himself (a changeling who reached Floor 11, per one recap).
- ✅ There are **goblins** outside the Tutorial Guild early on.

### Loot boxes and Donut talking (later Floor 1)
- ✅ Carl's loot boxes include a **Goblin Pass** and an **Enhanced Pet Biscuit**.
- ✅ Donut snatches and eats the biscuit, becomes sapient, and talks for the first time (around chapter 6 in one recap).
- ✅ Donut's own boxes include a **Magic Missile** tome and an enchanted crown she puts on before reading what it does (around chapter 7). Later she gets a gold box with the **Talisman of the Slate Butterfly** (around chapter 9).
- ✅ Donut quickly becomes a fan favourite with the galactic audience.

Chapter numbers come from fan recaps and may not match every edition.

## Game scenes vs. the book

| Scene id | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `prologue` | Canon-based | Window, car, collapse, head, cold, stairwell. The quick-time moves themselves are game invention. |
| `stairwell` | Invented | Crumbling step, rat swarm. Filler set pieces for the descent. Rework or cut. |
| `registration` | Canon-based | Rules, achievements, boxes, timer are from the book. The slab nearly crushing Carl is game invention (the stairwells do close). |
| `goblins` | Loosely canon | Goblins are near the Tutorial Guild in the book, but this fight is invented. |
| `lair` | Invented | A dragon is not the Floor 1 boss. Placeholder. |
